About Simply Savvy

At Simply Savvy Charcuterie, we believe great food brings people together. Based in Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin, we specialize in beautifully curated charcuterie boards, grazing tables, and custom catering designed to elevate every occasion.

From intimate gatherings and date nights to weddings, bridal showers, baby showers, corporate events, and celebrations of all sizes, each arrangement is thoughtfully crafted using fresh, high-quality ingredients and an eye for detail.

Our goal is simple: to create memorable experiences through food that is as beautiful as it is delicious. Whether you're looking for an elegant grazing table, a custom specialty board, or individual charcuterie cups, we're dedicated to making your event feel special from the first bite to the last.

Thank you for supporting our small business. We look forward to being part of your next celebration.

Behind Simply Savvy

Simply Savvy Charcuterie was founded in 2025 by Savannah as a creative outlet that combined her love for beautiful presentation, thoughtful details, and bringing people together. What began as a passion project quickly grew into a woman-owned business dedicated to helping others celebrate life's special moments.

With an eye for design and a passion for creating memorable experiences, Savannah carefully curates each board and grazing table using fresh, high-quality ingredients. Every creation is thoughtfully crafted to feel both elevated and approachable—balancing beautiful presentation with flavors guests will genuinely enjoy.

At Simply Savvy Charcuterie, every detail is rooted in creativity, connection, and a love for gathering around good food. From intimate get-togethers to weddings, showers, and celebrations of all kinds, each board is designed with the goal of creating something as memorable as the occasion itself.
